# Thrust levers, reverse levers, FLEX and A/THR instinctive disconnect

The four thrust levers, the two piggy-back reverse levers (engines 2 and 3) and the two red A/THR
instinctive-disconnect buttons, with FlyByWire's FADEC thrust rating, FLEX take-off thrust and the 15 s
disconnect inhibit. Native code: `native/avionics/a380x_detent_lever.h` (XPLM-free detented lever,
shared), `a380x_thrust.h` (XPLM-free thrust/FADEC core), `a380x_cockpit_lever.inc` (X-Plane side of a
detented cockpit lever with click sound, shared) and `a380x_thrust.inc` (adapter), unity-included from
`a380x_avionics.c`. Geometry: `tools/cockpit_lever_geometry.py` (shared) and
`tools/thrust_lever_cockpit.py`. Nothing here has been flown in X-Plane yet.

## Why the levers followed the cursor (findings)

What the cockpit had before this change (`config/cockpit_controls.json`, generated by
`tools/build_cockpit_controls.py` into `a380x_cockpit_controls.obj`):

- Each thrust lever was an `ATTR_manip_drag_axis` on X-Plane's `throttle_ratio[i]` along a straight
  0.195 m axis `(0, -0.002, -0.195)`. The cockpit object is attached rotated 180 deg, so object +Z is
  forward and that axis points **aft**: pulling the hand toward the pilot increased thrust, pushing it
  forward reduced it. The authored lever rotates 55.6 deg about a lateral pivot, so the drag never
  followed the lever.
- The hit boxes (`LEVER_THROTTLE_n_CLICK`) were static at the idle position, and the lever meshes had no
  animation at all (`docs/COCKPIT_CONTROLS.md`: "throttle travel is not yet animated"). The only visible
  result of a drag was the X-Plane throttle value (EWD N1, engine sound) tracking the projection of the
  cursor on that straight axis.
- The reverse levers were the same kind of straight drag (`+Z 0.12 m`, value 0 to -1) on
  `throttle_jet_rev_ratio`, also static.
- There is no `ATTR_manip_wheel`, no hover/device manipulator on the pedestal, no plugin code reading
  the mouse for the throttles and no FlyWithLua script writing them.
- X-Plane's joystick preferences (read only) assign an axis to throttle (`_joy_AXIS_use2 4` in
  `Output/preferences/X-Plane Joystick Settings.prf`); X-Plane writes `throttle_ratio` from it whenever
  it moves, so it also moves "the throttles".

The OBJ cannot make X-Plane move a drag manipulator without a held button, so the remaining in-sim
explanations are outside the OBJ (for instance a macOS trackpad "drag lock" / three-finger drag that
keeps the button logically down). This change removes every OBJ-side cause: the new manipulators are
`ATTR_manip_drag_rotate` (X-Plane click-hold-drag) about each lever's own pivot, animated with the lever
so the hit box stays on the handle, writing plugin datarefs that only the plugin turns into lever
positions.

## Levers (X-Plane mechanism)

| Part | Manipulator (controls OBJ, draw-disabled box animated with the part) | Dataref written | Shown by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thrust lever 1-4 | `ATTR_manip_drag_rotate` about the lever pivot, angle 0..55.x deg = 0..45 TLA, `ATTR_manip_keyframe` 25 and 35 at the 55 % / 78 % angles of the 3D table | `a380x/xp12/thrust/lever_N_input` | `lever_N_anim` (percent) |
| Reverse lever 2/3 | `ATTR_manip_drag_rotate` about the reverse lever pivot, 0..94 deg, nested in its thrust lever | `reverse_N_input` (0 stowed .. 1 MAX REV) | `reverse_N_anim` |
| Disconnect left / right | `ATTR_manip_command button` | command `athr_disconnect_left` / `_right` | `disc_left_anim` / `disc_right_anim` |

The pivot and axis of each `drag_rotate` are those of the authored motion and lie on the rotation axis,
so they are identical whether X-Plane evaluates them in object space or in the animated frame (Laminar's
A330 places its throttle `drag_rotate` inside the lever animation the same way).

**Why no X-Plane native detents.** `ATTR_axis_detent_range` with `drag_rotate` models a lever that must
be *lifted* (dragged radially, `lift` / `dataref2`) to leave a detent pit, as Laminar's A330 flap lever.
Airbus thrust levers have no lift; their CL and FLX/MCT detents are gates passed with more force. The
stickiness is therefore native (`a380x_detent_lever.h`) behind a native click-hold-drag. The shared
geometry helper still emits `ATTR_axis_detent_range` for levers that do lift (flaps).

**Detent model** (`a380x_detent_lever.h`, values in `a380x_thrust.h`):

| | IDLE | CL | FLX/MCT | TOGA | stowed | IDLE REV | MAX REV |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Position | 0 | 25 | 35 | 45 | 0 | 0.3 (= TLA -6) | 1 (= TLA -20) |
| Kind | end stop | sticky | sticky | end stop | end stop | sticky | end stop |
| Capture / escape | - | 1.0 / 3.5 deg TLA | 1.0 / 3.5 deg TLA | - | - | 0.04 / 0.10 | - |
| Click band in / re-arm | 0.5 / 1.5 deg | same | same | same | 0.02 / 0.06 | same | same |

A hand reaching or crossing a sticky detent captures the lever exactly in it; the lever stays there until
the hand is more than the escape distance away (about 1 cm of hand travel at the knob between CL and
FLX), then jumps to the hand ("breaks out"). A detent crossed with the hand already beyond the escape
distance in one frame (a deliberate fast push) is passed. A drag ends 0.25 s after the manipulator's
last write; the input dataref reports the hand while dragging and the lever otherwise, so the next
click-hold starts where the lever is shown. Values within 1e-6 of an end stop are the stop.

**Interlocks.** A reverse lever moves only with its thrust lever at IDLE; a thrust lever leaves IDLE only
with its reverse lever stowed; engines 1 and 4 have no reverser; in flight X-Plane is kept in forward
idle with the reverse lever pulled (FBW floors TLA at 0 in flight). The levers never move under A/THR.

**X-Plane handles.** The lever is written into X-Plane's own pilot handle,
`sim/cockpit2/engine/actuators/throttle_ratio` (the calibrated ratio of `fcu-calibration.ini`, IDLE as a
true 0) and `prop_mode` (3 = reverse, with `throttle_ratio` the reverse amount 0 at IDLE REV .. 1 at MAX
REV), only when the cockpit lever changed. The FCU, EWD, FWS and the legacy flight phase keep reading
the same handle.

**Hardware axes.** A change of `throttle_ratio` / `prop_mode` the plugin did not write (hardware axis,
keyboard/joystick commands, another plugin) is adopted as the lever position without stickiness (the
calibration tolerance snaps it into a detent, as before) and is never written back, so an axis is not
fought; detent entries from hardware also click, as FBW plays the sound on position. X-Plane's reverse
toggle is accepted on engines 2/3 only with their levers at IDLE (amount = the hardware axis from IDLE
REV to MAX REV) and refused (restored) on engines 1/4, in flight or with the lever forward; while an inner
engine reverses, the outer levers are held at IDLE (a single hardware axis then drives the reverse
amount). FLEX with hardware: put the hardware lever in its FLX/MCT detent (within the calibrated
tolerance); the 15 s inhibit also counts a held hardware binding of `a380x/xp12/fcu/athr_disconnect`.

**Keyboard and joystick-button throttles.** X-Plane's step commands (`sim/engines/throttle_up`,
`throttle_down` and their `_1` .. `_4` forms) are tapped, not replaced. While one is held, a lever
that reaches (within the calibration tolerance) or crosses CL or FLX/MCT stops in that detent until the
key is released; the next press moves it on (up: IDLE → CL → FLX/MCT → TOGA, down the same way back).
Without this, a keyboard lever usually landed a degree or two beside CL. It looked like CL in 3D, but
A/THR stayed in manual thrust. Bindable commands
`a380x/xp12/thrust/levers_idle`, `levers_cl`, `levers_flx_mct`, `levers_toga`, `levers_detent_up` and
`levers_detent_down` put all four levers into a detent directly (with the click). A hardware axis is still
adopted without stickiness.

## Detent click

`native/avionics/sounds/thrust_detent_click.wav` (mono PCM16, 44.1 kHz, 80 ms) is an original synthesis by
`tools/build_detent_click.py` (deterministic; `--check` in the build): a magnet snap, three damped
metallic partials, a 210 Hz pedestal thunk and a seating bounce. It plays once per detent entry of any
lever (CL, FLX/MCT, TOGA, IDLE, IDLE REV, MAX REV, stowed), at most one per frame (four levers moved
together by one hardware axis sound as one click), positioned at the lever's handle on the pedestal
(FBW `SOUND_LEVER_THROTTLE` node, relative to the pilot eye point as the ELEC panel sounds), on X-Plane's
interior bus, never while paused, in replay or with the plugin disabled. A missing file only silences it.

## How thrust reached the engines, and the FADEC now

**Before:** manual thrust was X-Plane's own throttle: the lever ratio went straight into the engine
(`ENGN_thro` = `throttle_ratio`; this ACF has no X-Plane FADEC notches, `acf/_fadec_target` and
`_fadec_tla` are zero, and `_throttle_drives_N2 1`), so CL gave 0.556 of X-Plane throttle and FLX/MCT
0.778, unrelated to any rating; FLEX existed only as an EWD display. A/THR overrode the engines
(`override_throttles` + `ENGN_thro_use`) with a normalised command capped at 0.88 at CL.

**Now (`fadec_mode` 1, default):** each engine's FBW N1 command is computed from its TLA
(`A380FadecComputer`): TLA 0..25 IDLE->CLB, 25..35 CLB->(FLEX or MCT), 35..45 (FLEX or MCT)->TOGA;
reverse |TLA| 6..20 IDLE+1->REV; in flight TLA floored at 0; IAS < 60 kt keep-out band 62.5..73.5 % N1 and
IAS < 35 kt N1 <= 76.5 %. The limits are the FBW tables already vendored for the EWD
(`a380x_ewd_fadec.cpp`), at the X-Plane OAT, pressure, pressure altitude, Mach, packs and anti-ice.

The derate reaches X-Plane as the **N1 fraction** of the command between the FADEC's IDLE and TOGA N1:
`ENGN_thro_use = (N1_cmd - N1_idle) / (N1_toga - N1_idle)` (reverse: between IDLE+1 and REV), through
`override_throttles`, while A/THR is not driving the engines. TOGA therefore keeps X-Plane's full rated
throttle (the flight model's take-off performance is unchanged) and FLEX/MCT/CLB are the FBW fractions
below it, whatever X-Plane's own N1 scale is. Example at sea level, ISA, packs off: TOGA 83.4 %, FLEX 60 C
78.2 % -> 0.92 of the throttle. A stopped engine gets the raw handle, so starts and shutdowns are unchanged.
X-Plane's N1-versus-throttle curve is not linear (throttle drives N2), so the displayed N1 at FLX is not
exactly the FLX limit line; `fadec_mode` 2 adds an integral trim on the measured X-Plane N1 (FBW's own
closed loop, valid only if X-Plane's N1 is on the FBW scale), `fadec_mode` 0 restores the raw handle.
These are the writable `a380x/xp12/thrust/fadec_mode` values; the plugin starts in 1 (an aircraft
reload keeps the current value).

**FLEX:** the FLEX temperature is the native FMS PERF T.O entry (`a380x/xp12/fms/to_flex_temp_c`, FBW
encoding 0.1 = FLEX 0 C, valid with a healthy FMC). FLEX N1 = `limitN1` of the TO and GA rows with that
assumed temperature (FLEX column of the table, actual-temperature theta2 and bleed), blended by
`(Mach - 0.04) / 0.04`, at min(16600 ft, pressure altitude) (`a380x_ewd_fadec_flex_n1`). The FLEX rating
per engine is FBW's: candidate = FLEX temperature valid and above TAT
(`outside_air_LE_temp_degc`); on the ground the candidate alone; in flight the latch, set on the ground
at TLA 35 and cleared at TLA 25 or 45. So FLX/MCT on the ground without a valid FLEX above TAT gives MCT
N1; after a FLEX take-off CL or TOGA select MCT for good; leaving FLX without reaching CL/TOGA changes
nothing. The FLEX temperature is frozen while FLX is selected with every lever at 35, and after a FLEX
take-off the CLB limit stays at min(CLB, FLEX) until 10 s after CL and CLB exceeding FLEX.

**Rating shown = rating applied.** The EWD takes engine 1's FADEC thrust limit type and N1 from this
module (`ewd_rating_source`), the higher detent's rating between detents (FBW: on the ground TOGA unless
FLEX; in flight CLB / MCT or FLX / TOGA). This also corrects the EWD's FLX N1, which was the TOGA limit at
the assumed temperature. The guidance (`MAN FLX` / `MAN MCT` FMA and take-off detent A/THR arming)
receives the same FBW FLEX rating instead of its own FMS check.

## A/THR at the detents

Unchanged engagement logic (verified against the guidance tests): with A/THR armed, all levers at or
below CL make it active; any lever above CL leaves it armed with manual thrust (MAN TOGA / FLX / MCT /
THR). New: the A/THR authority is the lever's own FADEC N1 (FBW clamps the A/THR N1 to N1 of the TLA),
i.e. the same engine command a manual lever at that TLA gets, per engine in the FCU actuator path and as
the guidance THR CLB ceiling, instead of 0.88 normalised at CL. When A/THR becomes active the FCU takes
`override_throttles` from this module's lease (`fcu_thrust_link`), and gives it back when it releases;
a foreign owner of `override_throttles` is never taken over.

Landing ([AUTOLAND.md](AUTOLAND.md)): in an autoland the A/THR RETARD mode (FMA IDLE) ramps the engine
command to idle from 50 ft RA and holds it after touchdown even with the levers left at CL; setting
the levers to IDLE (or both inner reversers) disconnects A/THR as before. TOGA on approach arms A/THR
(MAN TOGA) for the go-around; back at CL A/THR is active in THR CLB while SRS is engaged.

## Instinctive disconnect

Clicking a red button animates it (authored glTF translation, 80 ms in / 100 ms out) and holds
`a380x/xp12/fcu/athr_disconnect` for as long as the mouse is held: the FCU disconnects A/THR on the press
and the FWS raises its existing voluntary `A/THR OFF` caution/memo, as before. The held time of that
command (from either button or any binding) is measured in simulated time; at 15 s
`a380x/xp12/thrust/athr_disabled` latches: A/THR is disconnected and cannot arm (pushbutton, take-off
detents) or engage, and alpha floor is inhibited.

**FMGC power-up in this port** = a rising edge of "at least one FMC healthy"
(`a380x/xp12/fms/fmc_a/b/c_healthy`) while on the ground, or an aircraft (re)load. That is where FBW's
FMC `initSimVars` sends `ATHR_RESET_DISABLE`.

## Unverified in the simulator

- The feel of the drag: X-Plane's mapping of mouse motion to `drag_rotate` angle, whether X-Plane reads
  the input dataref back during a drag, and whether 1.0 / 3.5 deg TLA capture/escape feel right.
- That X-Plane's joystick throttle axis only writes `throttle_ratio` when it moves (otherwise a
  hardware axis would always win over the cockpit lever, which is acceptable but different).
- The engine response: N1 reached at FLX/MCT/CL with the N1-fraction schedule; whether `fadec_mode` 2
  is preferable; reverse thrust levels; X-Plane's behaviour under a permanently held
  `override_throttles` (reverse with `ENGN_thro_use`, starts).
- The click: loudness, placement, and that X-Plane's interior bus is muted outside.
- Hit-box alignment and draw order in the cockpit; the reverse box sits just ahead of the handle box.
- The cause of the original "follows the cursor" report (see the findings).

## Deviations from FBW and decisions for the user

- The derate is applied as an N1 fraction of X-Plane's throttle (not FBW's closed N1 loop) because
  X-Plane's N1 scale for this ACF is unknown; TOGA stays full throttle. Decide after a flight whether to
  switch the default to `fadec_mode` 2, or to calibrate the ACF engine.
- FBW's low-speed rules (IAS < 35 kt N1 <= 76.5 %, keep-out 62.5..73.5 % below 60 kt) now also limit
  TOGA/FLEX at the start of the roll through the same fraction.
- Manual thrust at CL is now the CLB rating (about 0.95+ of X-Plane's throttle at low altitude), no
  longer 0.556; A/THR at CL is limited to the same value instead of 0.88.
- A/THR active range (user decision 2026-11-09): the A380 limits A/THR to IDLE..CL (all engines).
  Here it is active from above IDLE up to, but not including, the FLX/MCT detent. The lever's FADEC
  thrust is the ceiling, so between CL and FLX/MCT A/THR may use more than climb thrust. In the
  FLX/MCT and TOGA detents thrust stays manual (takeoff, go-around). FBW's per-FADEC engine-out
  range is not modelled.
- FBW's FADEC thrust lock (THR LK on A/THR loss with the levers in CL) is not modelled here.
- Hardware detent entries click (as FBW, sound on 3D position); with a noisy axis the 1 deg re-arm band
  prevents chatter.
- The A/THR disable reset is the port's FMC-healthy edge on the ground (FBW: the FMC's own init).
- `config/cockpit_controls.json` still lists the old throttle drag axes; regenerating the controls OBJ
  from it brings them back and `tools/thrust_lever_cockpit.py` retires them again.
