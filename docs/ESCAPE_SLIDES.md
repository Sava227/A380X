# A380X escape slides (installed 2026-11-15)

All 16 passenger doors have a 3D escape slide: the 10 main-deck doors and the 6 upper-deck doors. When a door whose slide is armed is opened, the slide drops out of the door as a folded pack. It then unfolds hinge by hinge down to the ground, and its tubes inflate as each section unfolds. The lower ECAM DOOR page shows **SLIDE** next to every armed door.

## How to use it

1. **Arm the slides:** use the flyPad doors page, the "ready for departure" preset, or the command `a380x/xp12/efb/slides_arm_all` (`slides_disarm_all` disarms). Only closed doors can be armed.
2. **Check the SD:** the DOOR page shows a white **SLIDE** beside each armed, closed door.
3. **Open an armed door:** use its inside or outside handle, or `sim/flight_controls/door_toggle_N`. The slide deploys once the door is out of the way: at 25 % of the door travel on the main deck, 35 % on the upper deck. It takes about 6 s.
4. **Stow:** close the door (the slide repacks in 3 s), or use `a380x/xp12/doors/slides_stow_all`.

The flyPad's own door buttons still refuse to open a door with an armed slide ("Disarm the … slide before opening the door"), so ground handling from the flyPad can't blow a slide by accident.

### Slide logic (A380XDoors)

- **Arming state:** the avionics/EFB one (`a380x/xp12/efb/slide_armed[16]`). The plugin latches it while the door is shut, because the avionics disarms a slide as soon as its door opens.
- **Deployment:** a door that opens with its slide latched armed deploys that slide. The log says `A380X slides: door N opened with its slide armed - slide deploying`.
- **Order:** the same as `door_open_ratio` 0-15. Even numbers are the left doors, odd numbers the right; 0-9 main deck, 10-15 upper deck.

### Avionics patch

- **Location:** arm64 slice, `_efb_door_intercept` at file offset `0x91854`. It is the "before" handler on `sim/flight_controls/door_toggle_1..16`, which returned 0 for an armed door and so blocked it.
- **Change:** it now starts with `mov w0, #1; ret`, so X-Plane always runs the door command. Only those 8 bytes differ in the arm64 slice. The earlier AP-stick and LCD-colour patches are kept.
- **Signing:** re-signed ad hoc.
- **Not patched:** the x86_64 slice and the Windows plugin still block armed doors.
- **Source fix for the next rebuild:** remove the veto in `efb_door_intercept()` (`native/avionics/a380x_efb_web.inc`).

### The slides

- **Shape:**
  - a grey girt platform at the sill;
  - a ramp of hinged segments with two side tubes, a centre lane divider, a head tube and a toe tube;
  - a run-out on the ground.
- **Sizes:**
  - main deck: 1.85 m wide, 34°, 11-12.5 m long;
  - upper deck: 2.05 m wide, 31°, 18-19 m long.
- **Path:** each slide follows the ground under the aircraft at rest, estimated from the tyre contact points plus about 12 cm of strut compression.
- **Obstacles:**
  - Each slide rests on the wing or belly fairing where it meets them from above, and may pass under the wing with clearance.
  - The obstacle height maps come from Blender ray casts of the wing, engine, pylon and belly objects (`tools/wing_heightmap.py` → `build/wing_heightmap.json`).
  - Slides keep clear of each other in plan view.
- **M3, the over-wing door:**
  - It runs aft alongside the fuselage over the wing root and drops off behind the trailing edge at 42°, as a fuselage-mounted over-wing slide does.
  - Its path passes under the U2 slide, which is about 3 m higher there. Every U2 path outward has to cross it.
- **Deployment, from `slide_ratio` 0 to 1:**
  - The ramp starts folded accordion-style below the platform.
  - The hinges open in sequence from the top.
  - Every tube is made of 4 strips that move out from its centre line, from 25 % of the radius to full, while its segment unfolds.
  - Everything is hidden below a ratio of 0.004.

## How it was checked

- **Renders:** Blender renders at deploy ratios 0.1, 0.35, 0.6 and 1 (`renders/`): every slide reaches the ground, U2 passes under the wing, M3 runs over the wing root.
- **OBJ check:** `validate_obj.py` passes (16,560 vertices, 2,070 draw groups, animation nesting depth 12).
- **Plugin mock test:**
  - an armed M2L deploys after the door is a quarter open and is fully out 6 s later, although the avionics disarms it as the door opens;
  - a disarmed door doesn't deploy;
  - an upper door waits until 35 % open;
  - `slides_stow_all` and closing the door repack the slides.
- **SD page:** the patched SD page, run in a browser with armed, disarmed and open doors (`renders/sd_door_slide.png`).
- **Not yet tested in X-Plane.**

## Limits

- **Ground height:** this is an estimate. On a heavy aircraft or sloped ramp the toe can float slightly above the ground, or sink slightly below it.
- **Flaps:** with the flaps down, the M3 slide can pass through the flap fairings. The height map was made with the flaps up.
- **Platforms only:** Windows and Intel Macs still block opening armed doors (avionics veto not patched there). The slide model and logic work on all platforms.
- **Shading:** the slides use a plain, flat-coloured texture.
